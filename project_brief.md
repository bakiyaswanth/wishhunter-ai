# My agent: WishHunter AI
One-liner: A conversational shopping intelligence agent that helps Indian consumers make confident purchasing decisions with cross-retailer offer comparison, price-history analysis, and personalized "Buy Now vs. Wait" recommendations.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User PIN code, budget constraints, urgency horizons (e.g. wait 2 weeks), bank card holdings (HDFC/ICICI), and tracked wishlist targets across sessions.
- Tools: search_product_offers, get_product_price_history, calculate_effective_price, evaluate_buy_or_wait, set_target_price, and get_wishlist.
- Catalog/UI: Wishlist catalog cards, side-by-side retailer offer comparison tables, and A2UI Verdict Cards (BUY NOW, WAIT, or INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE).
- Image gen: Personalized shopping category mood board or dream setup visualization.
- Sandbox: Statistical price trend calculation, 90-day price volatility, and discount confidence modeling.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for historical price volatility analysis and savings probability modeling.
